# CAMPUS FLAVOR PULSE
## A Dynamic Demand-Driven College Canteen Strategy

---

### 1. EXECUTIVE IDEA

**Strategy Name:** CAMPUS FLAVOR PULSE (Dynamic Demand-Driven Operations)  
**Core Concept:** *Predict → Batch → Serve → Track → Adapt*  

Campus Flavor Pulse replaces gut-feeling cooking with a low-tech, high-precision demand forecasting system. By staging food production into small, fresh micro-batches and leveraging high-margin, high-demand student favorites, the canteen maximizes gross profit and keeps food waste below **2%**—all while preserving a ₹1,500 emergency buffer.

### 5. DEMAND FORECASTING SYSTEM

The canteen uses the **"85-65 Rule"**, a visual spreadsheet-free matrix logged daily at 8:00 PM:

* **≥ 85% Sold:** Increase next stock quantity by **15%**.
* **65% – 84% Sold:** Increase next stock quantity by **5% – 10%**.
* **40% – 64% Sold:** Keep stock quantity **approximately the same**.
* **< 40% Sold:** Decrease next stock quantity by **20% – 30%**.

#### Why This Prevents Stockouts & Waste:
1. **Prevents Stockouts:** A high sell-through rate ($\ge 85\%$) signals unmet student demand, giving operational permission to scale up production capacity safely.
2. **Prevents Food Waste:** A low sell-through rate ($< 40\%$) immediately cuts future prep, stopping ingredient decay before capital is lost in the trash bin.

---

### 6. SMART BATCH SYSTEM

Food is never prepared in a single morning batch. Production is split into timed micro-batches:

* **Breakfast (08:00 - 10:30 AM):** Batch 1 (60%), Batch 2 (40%)
* **Lunch (12:30 - 02:30 PM):** Batch 1 (50%), Batch 2 (30%), Batch 3 (20%)
* **Evening Snacks (04:30 - 06:30 PM):** Batch 1 (60%), Batch 2 (40%)

**Trigger Protocol:** The second lunch batch is initiated **only at 1:15 PM** if 70% of Batch 1 is sold out. If sales lag below 50% at 1:15 PM, Batch 2 is scaled down or cancelled, avoiding cooked food waste.

### 7. CONTINGENCY PLAN

#### Scenario A: Demand Suddenly Surges (+40% unexpected crowd)
* **Action:** Deploy the **"3-Minute Express Menu"** (High-margin, zero-prep-delay items like Classic Maggi, Butter Toast, Masala Lemonade, and Omelettes).
* **Financial Impact:** Draw up to ₹750 from the ₹1,500 Reserve Fund to buy instant replenishables (bread, eggs, Maggi packs) from local wholesale vendors nearby.

#### Scenario B: Demand Plummets (-50% due to rain/festivals)
* **Action:** Freeze Batch 2 production immediately. Shift remaining perishable raw veggies into durable evening options (e.g., leftover boiled potatoes turned into Crispy Aloo Tikki/Pakoras).
* **Financial Impact:** Run a **"Flash Happy Hour"** (20% discount on remaining items after 5:00 PM) to recover raw material cost rather than losing 100% value to waste.

### 8. STUDENT SATISFACTION (NEAR-ZERO COST IMPROVEMENTS)

1. **"Today's Fresh Batch" Live Counter:** A ₹50 whiteboard displaying exact times food came out of the kitchen (e.g., *"Fresh Samosas out at 4:15 PM"*).
2. **WhatsApp / QR Micro-Feedback:** QR code stickers on tables linking to a 1-question Google Form: *"How was lunch today?"*
3. **Student Choice Polls:** Weekly Instagram/WhatsApp poll allowing students to vote on Friday's special meal (e.g., Veg Biryani vs. Chole Bhature).
4. **Transparent Portion & Price Board:** Explicit display stating exact weights/quantities (e.g., *"Rajma Bowl: 250g Rice + 150ml Rajma = ₹50"*).
5. **Combos & Micro-Addons:** Offer ₹5 extra add-ons (extra chutney, fried green chillies, papad) to boost average order value while raising satisfaction.

### 9. CREATIVE DIFFERENTIATOR

#### Feature Name: **CANTEEN DEMAND RADAR**

An ultra-lightweight operational ritual executed every evening at 7:00 PM in 4 steps:

1. **Step 1: Log Sales** — Manager logs remaining stock on a simple physical tally sheet.
2. **Step 2: Run 85/65 Rule** — Classifies items into *Up*, *Down*, or *Hold* categories.
3. **Step 3: Prep Prep-Sheet** — Calculates exact raw material pull for tomorrow morning.
4. **Step 4: Broadcast Menu** — Publishes tomorrow's discounted "Early Bird" combo on WhatsApp.
